Magpiary UI directions

Three clickable directions for the four core screens: library, item, search and activity. All three use the same entirely fictional demonstration library. The UX fixes below are built into every direction, so you can compare the look and feel without the fixes getting in the way.

Round 3 · C1+ (current)

C1+ · Gallery with piles, time sections, Nest and settings

C1's style, plus C3's piles (Piles / Everything) and C2's "saved today / this week" sections. Top bar, all 40 px tall, in three tiers: the one action (Add a post, by the search), where you are (Library · Archive · Trash), then tools (tile size, Nest with its egg-in-a-nest icon that wobbles while something is being read, Settings). The wall heading changes on every visit (seven of them, no counts). The "On the go" strip is a quiet dark band in both themes. The yellow "Mockup options" chip switches the library state (normal, no AI key, first run) and the layout. Matches the product as built through M4 (24 Oct).

Post opens
A near-full-screen card: the video gets 40% of the width at almost full height, with scene frames below it; the information gets 60%. Clicking a key moment highlights its frame and timecode. Counts sit under the title with Refresh beside them. Tabs: Summary (the caption first, short ones large and long ones folded with "Show the full caption", then the summary and key moments) · Links · Related · Details. Shows the piles it's in, Archive / Remove, shared N×, a Links panel (best and possible matches, where each came from, add / remove / find more), rich captions (links, @mentions, #hashtags that filter the wall), which key wrote it, how long each step took, and where its files live. Photo carousels and text posts with comments have their own players.
Piles and Related
Piles as the product makes them: every save is in one, a subtitle from what they share, "N new", Regroup everything. A pile page has an overview (sources, dates, what they share), rename, merge, choose saves to split off or remove, and delete. Related lives in the post card's Related tab (with the reason for each and Not related), with "See all related" for the full list.
Library, Archive, Trash
Archive means done: out of the library, still searchable. Trash keeps posts 30 days, with Restore and Empty trash. Filters: type, source, Has links, Needs you, and a #tag from any caption or tag.
Strip
Clicking "On the go" opens the Nest.
Nest
The product's Activity page: reading now (fetch, transcript, frames, summary, links), the two waiting lines, needs you with Retry now, pause switches for Transcribing, Summarising (all or the next N) and Finding links, Keep awake, a history of where each post's time went, and averages per source.
No AI key
A full-colour wall: labels in the source's colour, titles from the caption, a small "✦ Summarise" badge, and a friendly banner you can dismiss.
First run
A guided setup: a welcome with a live demo and a privacy explanation, pairing with checks that tick themselves off, Smart or Private (Smart with a live key check, or skip; Private downloads the Mac model), then the first share arriving and being read in front of you.
Layout
Boxed on screens wider than 1920 px by default, with the width set by a slider (Settings → Appearance); full width is the alternative. The Mockup options chip can force the box on any screen to preview it.
Motion
GSAP + Flip, vendored in vendor/gsap. Tiles morph when you filter, search or change size; a tile flies into the post card and back; pages enter with a short stagger; Nest bars grow and numbers count up; setup steps slide in and checks pop. Nothing moves when reduced motion is on.
Other states
Paste-a-link (shows the tracking it removes, then the post appears in Today and progresses), being read, waiting, couldn't open.
Settings
AI & summaries: pick Smart (your key reads the transcript and frames) or Private (a model on this Mac, nothing leaves), and only that choice's options show underneath. Smart: keys in order with limit states, add a key with a guide, only the next N, AI tags added automatically or only suggested, retry failed summaries. Private: the Mac model (download, standard or larger), when to read, limits and tags. Then Links (free directories, optional Brave key), Phones & this Mac (home Wi-Fi per phone, new install code, add a phone, keep awake, firewall note), Library & storage, Appearance. Other AI providers and the browser-login fallback aren't shown.

UX pass on the current hub

  1. Lead with the summary biggestThe summary is what Magpiary adds, but on the item page it sits under the caption in a side column, below the counts. Every direction puts the AI title and summary first. The caption, counts and timing move under "More".
  2. Better titles until a summary arrives"Video by examplecreator" and "Post by examplemaker" tell you nothing. Until the AI title is ready, use the caption's first line and mark it as a draft (shown in italics or grey).
  3. "Archive" means three different thingsIt's a shelf (the Archive tab), a fetch status ("archived") and the folder of saved media. Rename the shelf to Done, which is also what you'd press when you've finished with an item. "Archived" stays internal.
  4. One box, not twoThe top bar has a search box and a paste-a-link box side by side. A single box can do both: paste a URL and it offers "Add" (and shows the tracking it strips); type words and it searches.
  5. Browse by type, not by sourceThe source chips (Instagram 11, X 1…) and tag chips (#macos 1) take up two rows and are mostly noise. The AI already sorts items into How-to, Recipe, Tool, News and so on, which is how you'll actually look for things. Source becomes a small colour dot.
  6. Search should show where it matchedSearch already covers transcripts, but the results are just the same grid. Show the matched line ("Said at 0:31: …paper hinge…") and jump to that moment. This is Magpiary's killer feature, so make it visible.
  7. One pipeline vocabularyThe strip, the card badges and the Activity page each describe progress in different words. Use four steps everywhere: saved → fetched → watched → summarised. Directions B and C draw them as pips or columns.
  8. Activity: what's happening first, controls secondToday, Controls come first and "Working on now" second, and the "Average time per step" table is debug detail. Lead with now and next, plus an estimate ("about 2 minutes until clear"). Pause toggles become quiet switches, and timings move to the item or to a details view.
  9. "Needs attention" deserves an actionIt's a red chip among the source filters. Make it a note with Try now and the next retry time. It's the only thing in the app that asks anything of you.
  10. Empty tilesItems without a thumbnail show as blank dark cards. Use a typographic tile for these instead (caption or title on the source colour), as C does for text posts.
